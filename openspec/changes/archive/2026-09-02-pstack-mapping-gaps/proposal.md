## Why

Cursor pstack workflows and 21 principles map onto Gas City formulas, expansion
personas, and drains. Three pack-owned holes remain. Corpus-only skills look
like sling names. Interrogate judgment is not a producer gate. `pstack-work`
replaces inherited `do-work` worktree assets with stubs.

## What Changes

- Name corpus-only Cursor skills in `playbooks.toml`.
- Gate `pstack-interrogate` judgment with a review artifact path and the shared
  validator.
- Stop `pstack-work` from replacing `do-work` prepare-worktree and
  close-source-anchor assets.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `pstack-gascity-pack`: Corpus-only skills. Gated interrogate judgment.
  Implementation convoy inherits `do-work` worktree assets.
- `pstack-delivery-evidence`: Playbook map lists corpus-only skills.

## Impact

`pstack/mappings/playbooks.toml`, `pstack/formulas/pstack-interrogate.formula.toml`,
