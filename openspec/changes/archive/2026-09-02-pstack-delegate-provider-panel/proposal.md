## Why

Cursor arena and interrogate get N-model signal from host Task children.
The Gas City pstack pack must not copy that spawn. Sequential formulas with
inert `gc.graph_operator` also cannot bind N providers. City `[[rigs.patches]]`
is 1:1. The missing primitive is a city provider panel that Gas City expands.

## What Changes

- Record that N-model fanout is Gas City provider routing, not pack Task
  fields and not a `graph_operator` interpreter.
- Require pack formulas to stay providerless and to omit provider ids.
- Require isolated per-child artifact paths. Ban the shared
  `.gc/pstack/arena-candidate.md` binding once panel expansion ships.
- Forbid stamping `gc.provider_panel` until Gas City consumes it.
- Keep current sequential arena and interrogate graphs in this checkout.
- Record `[[provider_panels]]` cook in this repository OpenSpec.
- Record that Formula daemon model selection is frozen in `[providers.<id>] args`.
- Record that panel members are catalog ids, not model slugs.
- Record that sibling methodology packs fan personas, not models.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `gascity-provider-panel`: City table of harness ids. Gas City cooks N
  child beads and binds each to one registered provider.

### Modified Capabilities

- `pstack-gascity-pack`: Method formulas delegate N-model fanout to that
  panel. They do not invent a pack scheduler.
- `pstack-delivery-evidence`: README may name the panel target.
  Catalog strings and this checkout still MUST NOT claim executed
  multi-provider fanout.

## Impact

Affected trees are `pstack/` docs and repository `openspec/`.
Formula TOML in this checkout does not stamp the new key. Gas City cook
is a runtime consumer, not a sibling project.
